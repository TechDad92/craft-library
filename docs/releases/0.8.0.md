Focus mode is gone. Instead, the folder list and the preview can each be tucked away and brought back, just like the toolbar.

## Installing
- **If you have 0.7.0:** a note will appear in the bottom corner when you open Craft Library. Click **Update now** and it does the rest.
- **Otherwise:** download **Craft-Library-Setup-0.8.0.exe** below and run it. It updates Craft Library in place, and all settings, notes and colour labels are kept. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## What's new
- **Hide the folder list:** click the small arrow at the top of the folder list. A slim tab stays at the edge of the window; click it to bring the folder list back.
- **Hide the preview:** click the small arrow at the top of the preview. A slim tab stays at the right-hand edge of the window (or the bottom, if your preview sits at the bottom); click it to bring the preview back.
- Both are remembered for next time, and they're also in **Settings › Layout › Panels**, next to the toolbar.

## Changed
- **Focus mode has been taken out.** Hiding the toolbar, folder list and preview does the same job, and you choose which ones go.
- The preview's **Hidden** choice in Settings › Layout has gone, because the preview now has its own hide arrow. If your preview was hidden, it stays hidden: click the tab at the edge of the window to bring it back.
- The **Show preview** button above the files has gone too; the tab at the edge of the window does that now.
