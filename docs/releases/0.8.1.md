The folder list and the preview now have one small tab each, in the middle of their edge, for both hiding and showing.

## Installing
- **If you have 0.7.0 or newer:** a note will appear in the bottom corner when you open Craft Library. Click **Update now** and it does the rest.
- **Otherwise:** download **Craft-Library-Setup-0.8.1.exe** below and run it. It updates Craft Library in place, and all settings, notes and colour labels are kept. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## Changed
- **One tab to hide and show.** The small arrows at the top of the folder list and the preview have gone. Instead, a slim tab sits half-way down the edge of each panel, next to your files:
  - Click it to hide the panel. The tab stays at the edge of the window, so click it again to bring the panel back.
  - Its arrow always points the way the panel will go.
  - When the preview sits at the bottom of the window, its tab is in the middle of the preview's top edge.
- If the preview is in its own window, it still hides with the **Hide** link in that window, and the tab at the right-hand edge brings it back.
