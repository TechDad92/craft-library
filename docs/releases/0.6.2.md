Focus mode is calmer: it now hides the toolbar too.

## Installing
1. Download **Craft-Library-Setup-0.6.2.exe** below and run it. It updates Craft Library in place, and all settings, notes and colour labels are kept.
2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## Changed
- **Focus mode now hides the toolbar** as well as the folder list and search, leaving just your files.
  - To get the toolbar back while focusing, click the little tab under the middle of the top bar. Click **Focus mode** on the toolbar to leave Focus mode.
  - Pulling the toolbar down during Focus mode doesn't change your usual toolbar choice: when Focus mode ends, the toolbar goes back to how you normally have it.
