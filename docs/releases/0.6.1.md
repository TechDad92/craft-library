A simpler top bar: just the logo, name, search and window buttons.

## Installing
1. Download **Craft-Library-Setup-0.6.1.exe** below and run it. It updates Craft Library in place, and all settings, notes and colour labels are kept.
2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## Changed
- **Hiding and showing the toolbar has moved out of the top bar.**
  - To hide the toolbar, click the small arrow at its right-hand end.
  - To bring it back, click the little tab that hangs under the middle of the top bar.
  - The switch in Settings › Layout still works too.
- The top bar now holds only the logo, the name, the search box and the window buttons.
- With the toolbar hidden in Focus mode, pull the toolbar back down with the tab and click Focus mode to leave it.
