A tidier top banner: the Menu button and the search box now sit together in the middle.

## Installing
1. Download **Craft-Library-Setup-0.3.2.exe** below and run it. It updates Craft Library in place, and all settings, notes and colour labels are kept.
2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## Changed
- **The Menu button is now just left of the search box, and the two sit together in the middle of the banner.** The logo and name stay on the left, and the minimise, maximise and close buttons stay in the top-right corner.
- The Menu list now opens straight down from the Menu button.
- Buttons that only appear now and then (Home, Hidden, Exit focus and today's progress) sit on the right, next to the window buttons.
- On a small window the search box gets narrower, so nothing overlaps.
