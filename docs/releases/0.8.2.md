The toolbar now hides and shows the same way as the folder list and the preview: with one small tab in the middle.

## Installing
- **If you have 0.7.0 or newer:** a note will appear in the bottom corner when you open Craft Library. Click **Update now** and it does the rest.
- **Otherwise:** download **Craft-Library-Setup-0.8.2.exe** below and run it. It updates Craft Library in place, and all settings, notes and colour labels are kept. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## Changed
- **One tab for the toolbar.** The small arrow at the right-hand end of the toolbar has gone. Instead, a slim tab hangs under the middle of the toolbar:
  - Click it to hide the toolbar. The tab then hangs under the top bar, so click it again to bring the toolbar back.
  - Its arrow points the way the toolbar will go: up to hide it, down to bring it back.
